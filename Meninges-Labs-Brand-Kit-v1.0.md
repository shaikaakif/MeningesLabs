## 1. Executive summary

Meninges Labs is a digital growth, engineering and intelligence company.
It combines digital presence, custom engineering, search visibility,
performance marketing and AI-powered automation into tailored growth
systems for businesses.

Meninges Labs does not force every client into one fixed package. It
identifies the client's objectives, current infrastructure, acquisition
challenges and operational bottlenecks, then assembles a suitable
engagement from its capabilities.

> **We engineer the digital infrastructure that helps businesses become
> more visible, acquire customers, convert opportunities and operate
> more efficiently.**

The tagline, **The Architecture of Autopilot**, expresses the ambition
to connect fragmented digital activities into coordinated systems. It is
not a promise that a business can operate without human oversight.

## 2. Company positioning

**Category:** Digital Growth & Intelligence

-   Architecture and interior design firms.
-   Healthcare providers and clinics.
-   Manufacturing and industrial businesses.
-   Hospitality and other appointment- or enquiry-driven businesses.

### Ideal-client characteristics

-   Has a commercially valuable product or service.
-   Receives or wants to generate consistent enquiries.

## 5. Service architecture

Meninges Labs has five service divisions. These are capabilities from
which tailored client engagements are assembled---not five mandatory
items in every package.

### 01 --- Digital Engineering

**Purpose:** Build and improve the digital products and infrastructure
through which a business presents itself and operates.

Services: - Business websites and landing pages. - Website redesign and
redevelopment. - Custom SaaS platforms. - Business portals and
dashboards. - Native Android applications. - API development and
integrations. - Database-backed applications. - Technical maintenance
and performance improvements.

**Typical commercial model:** One-time discovery, design, engineering
and deployment fee; optional ongoing maintenance, hosting and support;
separately scoped enhancements.

### 02 --- Search Intelligence

**Purpose:** Improve how a business and its expertise are discovered
through search experiences.

Services: - Technical SEO and on-page SEO. - Local SEO and
business-profile optimisation. - Content strategy and content
architecture. - Structured data and schema implementation. - Answer
Engine Optimisation (AEO). - Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO). -
Search performance measurement and reporting.

**Typical commercial model:** Initial audit and implementation project,
followed by an optional monthly optimisation and reporting retainer.
Content production or larger technical work may be scoped separately.

SEO, AEO and GEO are optimisation disciplines, not guarantees of
rankings, AI citations or traffic. Results depend on competition,
content quality, authority, technical conditions and platform changes.

### 03 --- Performance Marketing

**Purpose:** Generate relevant demand through paid media and improve
campaign efficiency.

Services: - Meta advertising across Facebook and Instagram placements. -
Google Ads, including relevant Search and other campaign formats. -
ChatGPT advertising, subject to platform availability, advertiser
eligibility and applicable policies. - Campaign strategy and account
setup. - Audience and conversion strategy. - Ad creative direction and
production coordination. - Landing-page and conversion-rate
optimisation. - Tracking, attribution and reporting.

**Typical commercial model:** Initial campaign setup fee where
applicable; monthly campaign-management retainer; advertising spend paid
by the client to the platform wherever practical; substantial creative
production or additional landing pages scoped separately.

Advertising spend is not Meninges Labs revenue when paid directly by the
client to the platform. Never imply that paid advertising guarantees
leads, sales or a particular return.

### 04 --- AI & Automation

**Purpose:** Reduce repetitive work and improve the speed and
consistency of business workflows.

Services: - AI agents and assistants. - Inbound and outbound voice
agents. - Lead qualification workflows. - Enquiry response and
routing. - CRM automation. - Appointment and site-visit scheduling. -
Calendar integrations. - Follow-up workflows. - Internal operational
automations. - Monitoring, evaluation and ongoing improvement.

**Typical commercial model:** 1. One-time discovery, configuration,
integration, testing and deployment fee. 2. Monthly subscription or
managed-service fee for hosting, monitoring, maintenance and support. 3.
Usage-based charges for telephony, call minutes, speech recognition,
speech generation, model tokens, messaging and other third-party
consumption. 4. Clearly defined included usage, overage rates and
service limits.

Agents must use approved business information and real system
availability. They must not invent appointment slots, make unauthorised
commitments or misrepresent themselves as human. Calling, recording,
messaging and personal-data workflows must comply with applicable
consent, privacy and telecom requirements.

### 05 --- Growth Intelligence & Consulting

**Purpose:** Diagnose business problems, set priorities and coordinate
improvements across the other divisions.

Services: - Digital presence and infrastructure audits. - Growth
strategy and roadmaps. - Customer journey analysis. - Lead-funnel
analysis. - Analytics and reporting. - Conversion-rate optimisation. -
Digital transformation consulting. - Performance reviews and
optimisation planning.

**Typical commercial model:** Fixed-fee audit or strategy engagement,
project-based consulting, or ongoing advisory/performance retainer.

## 6. Tailored engagement and packaging model

Meninges Labs uses a **modular, needs-based packaging model**. A client
does not automatically purchase every service division. The engagement
is assembled from the services that address the client's objectives and
constraints.

### Engagement design process

1.  **Discover:** Understand the business, offer, audience, goals and
    current systems.
2.  **Diagnose:** Identify acquisition, conversion, visibility and
    operational bottlenecks.
3.  **Prioritise:** Separate essential work from optional improvements.
4.  **Design:** Select relevant capabilities and define dependencies.
5.  **Scope:** Specify deliverables, exclusions, timeline, client
    responsibilities and acceptance criteria.
6.  **Price:** Separate one-time engineering, recurring management,
    third-party costs and usage.
7.  **Deploy:** Build, integrate, test and launch.
8.  **Operate:** Monitor systems and deliver agreed recurring services.
9.  **Optimise:** Review evidence and improve the system within the
    agreed scope.

### Example: Real Estate Growth System

A real estate client may need: - A redesigned property website and
campaign landing pages. - Technical and local SEO foundations. - Search
content and structured data. - Meta lead-generation campaigns. - An AI
voice assistant to respond to opted-in enquiries. - Lead qualification
and CRM updates. - Real calendar availability checks. - Confirmed
property site-visit appointments. - Reminder and follow-up workflows. -
Reporting on qualified leads, booked visits and attendance.

This is an illustrative solution architecture, not a universal package
or a promise of performance.

## 7. Commercial and revenue architecture

Meninges Labs uses different charging models according to the nature of
the work.

### A. One-time project fees

Suitable for website builds and redesigns, SaaS and application
development, initial SEO or analytics implementation, campaign setup, AI
agent development and integration, audits and strategy projects.

### B. Monthly recurring retainers

Suitable for SEO and AEO/GEO management, performance marketing
management, website maintenance, AI agent monitoring and maintenance,
CRM and workflow support, analytics, reporting and optimisation.

### C. Subscriptions / managed infrastructure

Suitable for systems requiring ongoing hosting, availability,
maintenance, support and platform management.

### D. Usage-based charges

Suitable for variable third-party consumption, such as voice and
telephony minutes, AI model usage and tokens, speech-to-text and
text-to-speech, SMS and messaging, external API calls and other metered
services.

### Commercial transparency rules

Every proposal should make clear: - What the one-time fee includes. -
What the monthly fee includes. - What usage allowance is included. -
What is billed as overage. - Which third-party accounts are
client-owned. - Which expenses are paid directly by the client. - What
support and response times are included. - What happens when usage
limits are reached. - Renewal, cancellation and handover terms.

Advertising budgets should normally be paid directly by clients to
advertising platforms. Third-party costs must not be hidden inside an
apparently unlimited service.

### Unit economics discipline

Calculate project profitability after assigning a realistic cost to
engineering time, contractor time, tools, deployment, QA, project
management and other delivery costs.

**Gross profit = Service revenue − Direct delivery costs**

**Gross margin = Gross profit ÷ Service revenue**

Track one-time project economics separately from recurring-service
economics. Do not count client-funded advertising expenditure as agency
revenue when it is merely a pass-through payment.

## 10. Logo direction

The logo should communicate a credible, sophisticated engineering
company.

Preferred exploration: - A distinctive architectural or geometric
monogram. - A custom, premium geometric sans-serif wordmark. - Organic
sculptural geometry only where it improves the symbol. - Flat, precise
master artwork. - Strong legibility at favicon and application-icon
sizes.

Avoid generic AI nodes, literal brain illustrations, unnecessary orbital
or infinity motifs, glass effects as part of the essential logo,
childish pixel-art styling and excessive detail that disappears at small
sizes.

The intended identity is one recognisable master symbol, with the full
company-name wordmark developed as a companion. Small-size versions may
be optically simplified while preserving the same identity.

## 11. Website and digital experience direction

### Primary objective

The company website must explain what Meninges Labs does, whom it
serves, how its services connect, what outcomes it works toward and how
a prospect can begin an engagement. It should also demonstrate the
company's own standards of engineering and digital experience.

### Visual direction

A fusion of: 1. **Editorial luxury:** oversized typography, thoughtful
whitespace and refined composition. 2. **Botanical-inspired visual
warmth:** the approved warm light palette and rich natural pigments. 3.
**Architectural minimalism:** proportion, structure, grids and
precision. 4. **Selective experimental elements:** 3D sculptures,
cinematic photography and carefully controlled glass surfaces.

### Motion direction

-   Cinematic, fluid and sophisticated.
-   Purposeful scroll-triggered reveals.
-   Carefully choreographed transitions.
-   Touch-responsive interactions.
-   No hover-only essential functionality.
-   Reduced-motion support.
-   Performance-conscious animation.

### Mobile-first principle

Mobile is the primary design canvas. Layouts should be designed for
touch and narrow screens rather than merely shrinking a desktop design.

The previously discussed estimate that 95% of visitors may be on mobile
is not verified. Measure actual traffic after launch.

### Proposed website structure

-   Home.
-   Services.
-   Solutions / Industries.
-   Engagement approach.
-   Work / Case studies.
-   Insights.
-   About.
-   Start a project.

### Website credibility rules

Show actual work and functioning demonstrations. Publish only verified
client counts, project counts, testimonials and outcomes. Clearly label
concept demonstrations. Do not fabricate rankings, AI citations,
campaign performance or customer results. Explain service scope and
engagement models clearly.

## 13. Client delivery standards

Every engagement should have: - A discovery record and agreed business
objective. - A written scope of work. - A deliverables list and
exclusions. - A timeline and approval process. - Named client and
delivery responsibilities. - A test and acceptance checklist. - Access
and account-ownership documentation. - Security and data-handling
expectations. - A handover record. - A support and maintenance agreement
where applicable. - A reporting plan for ongoing engagements.

For AI systems, additionally document approved use cases, human
escalation paths, knowledge sources, tool permissions, usage limits,
logging and monitoring, failure and fallback behaviour, and
consent/opt-out handling where applicable.
