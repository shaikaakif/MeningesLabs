## 1. Positioning: what should people find?

MENINGES LABS should be presented consistently as an **AI-powered digital growth and technology studio** that builds:

- SaaS products and custom software.
- Websites and digital experiences.
- Applied AI solutions.
- Intelligent workflow automations.
- Digital growth systems, including SEO, AEO, GEO and performance marketing such as Meta Ads.
- Digital strategy and consulting.

The core idea is that these capabilities can work together as a connected system. Do not describe the company as only a web-design agency, only an SEO agency, or only an AI chatbot provider.

**Core positioning sentence:**

> MENINGES LABS builds SaaS products, websites, applied AI solutions and intelligent automations, combining engineering and digital growth strategy to help businesses work smarter and grow.

Only publish a service page when MENINGES LABS is genuinely ready to deliver that service. Do not imply guaranteed search rankings, leads, sales, ROAS, AI citations or business outcomes.

## 3. Local and “near me” searches: important reality check

Examples such as “AI agency near me”, “website developer near me” and “digital marketing agency near me” are location-sensitive. Google says local results are mainly influenced by **relevance, distance and prominence**. A website cannot force a “near me” position merely by repeating those words.

Because MENINGES LABS has said it does not have a customer-facing offline office, do **not** invent an address or create a misleading Google Business Profile. Google's eligibility guidance says online-only businesses that do not make in-person contact with customers are not eligible for a Business Profile.

A legitimate Hyderabad strategy can still include a useful Hyderabad-focused service page if the business genuinely serves clients there. It should:

- State the actual service coverage and whether work is remote, in-person by arrangement, or both.
- Explain relevant services in a Hyderabad business context without pretending to have an office in every locality.
- Include genuine local examples, original insights or real project evidence when available.
- Provide clear contact and enquiry options.
- Avoid creating nearly identical pages for Attapur, Gachibowli, Hitech City, Madhapur and every other locality just to capture queries.

Do not create a separate doorway page for every variation of “near me”.

## 6. Answer-led FAQs to add to the right service pages

These are example questions and draft answer directions. Keep answers accurate, specific and consistent with the actual services and delivery model.

1. **What does an AI-powered digital agency do?**  
   It combines digital strategy and technology capabilities such as software, websites, applied AI and automation. Explain how MENINGES LABS scopes and connects those capabilities.

2. **Does MENINGES LABS build custom SaaS products?**  
   Describe the SaaS and software development work the team is prepared to deliver. Explain discovery, MVP scope, architecture, development and deployment.

3. **Can you build a website for an established business?**  
   Explain the website process: goals, content, structure, design, development, testing, launch and maintenance scope.

4. **Can AI automate our existing business workflows?**  
   Explain that suitability depends on the workflow, tools, data, risk and need for human approval. Offer a discovery conversation rather than promising universal automation.

5. **Do you provide SEO services in Hyderabad?**  
   State the actual geographic service coverage and explain the SEO scope. Do not imply an office location if none exists.

6. **What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?**  
   SEO improves search discoverability; AEO makes useful answers easier to identify and understand; GEO focuses on visibility in generative AI experiences. Explain their overlap.

7. **Can you help a business appear in ChatGPT search?**  
   Explain crawler access, clear content, strong references and technical accessibility. Explicitly state that no agency can guarantee a ChatGPT mention or citation.

8. **Do you manage Facebook and Instagram advertisements?**  
   Explain the actual Meta Ads service scope, campaign planning, creative testing, tracking and reporting. Keep media spend separate from management fees.

9. **How do you measure digital marketing performance?**  
   Explain relevant metrics such as qualified enquiries, conversion rate, cost per qualified lead and agreed campaign outcomes. Do not invent benchmarks.

10. **How does a project with MENINGES LABS begin?**  
    Explain the initial enquiry, business context, discovery, scope, proposal and agreed next steps.

11. **Do you work with startups and established businesses?**  
    Describe the client types the company genuinely intends and is equipped to serve.

12. **Can your services work together?**  
    Explain how a website, search visibility, paid acquisition, software and automation may form one connected system when the business needs them.

13. **Do you provide ongoing support after launch?**  
    State the support and maintenance options actually available; do not imply a blanket support agreement.

14. **How much does a website or SaaS project cost?**  
    Explain that price depends on scope, complexity, integrations, content and ongoing requirements. Publish actual packages only when pricing is decided.

15. **How long does a project take?**  
    Explain that timelines depend on requirements, approvals, content and integrations. Give estimates only after project discovery.

16. **Do you have an office in Hyderabad?**  
    Be transparent: MENINGES LABS does not currently list a customer-facing office. Explain the real working and meeting arrangements without inventing an address.

17. **How can I discuss a project?**  
    Direct visitors to the official contact page and `info@meningeslabs.com`.

18. **What makes a good automation candidate?**  
    Explain repetitive, rules-based or information-heavy tasks; also explain where exceptions, risk or judgement require people.

19. **Can you integrate AI with existing software?**  
    Explain that integrations depend on available APIs, permissions, data quality, privacy and technical feasibility.

20. **What happens to our data when using an AI solution?**  
    Explain that data handling must be scoped and documented for the chosen tools and providers. Do not make blanket security or compliance claims without evidence.

## 8. Tracking and validation

Set up and use:

- **Google Search Console:** monitor queries, impressions, clicks, average position, indexing and page performance.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools:** monitor Bing visibility and crawling.
- **Analytics:** measure visits, enquiry clicks and submitted leads with privacy-conscious configuration.
- **Lead tracking:** distinguish a page visit from an actual qualified enquiry.

Create a monthly report with:

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Query impressions | Shows which searches trigger the site |
| Organic clicks | Measures visits from search |
| Landing-page performance | Identifies pages attracting relevant visitors |
| Search position trend | Helps track visibility, not business success by itself |
| Indexed important URLs | Checks whether core pages are eligible and discovered |
| Enquiry clicks and leads | Connects search activity to commercial outcomes |
| Qualified lead rate | Indicates whether the traffic matches the intended audience |
| AI referral traffic/citations | Tracks observable AI discovery without treating individual responses as rankings |

**Keyword validation process:**
1. Add the site to Google Search Console and verify the domain.
2. Review the Queries report once enough data has accumulated.
3. Compare the real queries with the clusters in this playbook.
4. Use Google Keyword Planner or another reputable keyword tool to estimate demand and competition.
5. Prioritise terms by service fit, intent, ability to provide a genuinely useful answer and realistic competition—not volume alone.
6. Review pages after publication and improve based on actual search and enquiry data.

## 9. Implementation checklist for the developer

- [ ] Confirm the final service list and which services are actively offered.
- [ ] Map one primary search intent to each important page.
- [ ] Write unique title tags and meta descriptions.
- [ ] Ensure each page has one clear H1 and a logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- [ ] Add descriptive internal links between related services, guides and contact pages.
- [ ] Use descriptive image filenames and useful alt text for meaningful images; use empty alt text for purely decorative images.
- [ ] Add accurate Organization, WebSite, Person and relevant page-level structured data.
- [ ] Keep structured data consistent with visible page content.
- [ ] Ensure canonical URLs, sitemap.xml and robots.txt are correct.
- [ ] Ensure important pages can be crawled and are not accidentally `noindex`.
- [ ] Add analytics events for WhatsApp, email and project-enquiry actions.
- [ ] Test mobile layout, accessibility, page speed and production metadata.
- [ ] Validate structured data and monitor Search Console after deployment.

## 10. What not to do

- Do not create dozens of near-identical city or neighbourhood pages with only place names swapped.
- Do not repeat “AI agency Hyderabad” unnaturally across every section.
- Do not publish FAQs that the company cannot answer truthfully.
- Do not invent office addresses, Google reviews, client logos, testimonials, case studies, certifications, prices or performance metrics.
- Do not promise first-page rankings, guaranteed leads, guaranteed Meta Ads ROAS or guaranteed AI citations.
- Do not publish AI-generated articles at scale without review, original insight and factual validation.
- Do not create a Google Business Profile for an online-only business that does not meet in-person eligibility requirements.
